# Form Check language

Form Check describes what one camera can observe during a practice session. These terms keep visible movement separate from inferred basketball events.

## Language

**Movement mode**:
The exercise or posture pattern selected for a session, such as a squat, kettlebell swing, or basketball shot.

**Tracked rep**:
A complete visible movement cycle recognized in an exercise mode. It does not certify safe technique.

**Shot motion**:
A visible movement of the shooting arm from a set-like position to extension. It does not establish that a ball was released.
_Avoid_: Shot attempt, shot

**Release**:
An observed separation of a detected ball from the shooting hand during an upward shot sequence. Arm extension alone is insufficient.

**Shot outcome**:
A classification of what happened at the rim, including an explicit unknown result when a single camera cannot distinguish a make from a miss.

**Session**:
One calibrated period of practice followed by a review of the measurements the camera could support.
